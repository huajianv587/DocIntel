# Demo sustainability report fixture

This is a self-authored fixture, not a company report. In fiscal year 2024 the example company reported Scope 1 emissions of 120 tCO2e and Scope 2 emissions of 80 tCO2e. The report says these values are operational metrics for the stated reporting period and should not be compared with another entity without checking boundaries and methodology.

The example company also states a target to reduce operational emissions by 30 percent by 2030 against a 2020 baseline. The target is a stated commitment, not evidence that the reduction has already been achieved.
